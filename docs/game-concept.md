# Game concept

Commonwealth is an economy idle game where progress becomes new settlements, tools, and possibilities. Its aim is to keep growth understandable instead of relying entirely on increasingly abstract money totals.

## Agreed direction

- **Settlement archetypes:** Purchase multiple specialized producers, such as food settlements, lumber camps, and mining outposts.
- **A shared economy:** Resources are pooled. Players do not configure transport routes or micromanage individual settlement logistics.
- **Interconnected progression:** Resources unlock improvements elsewhere. Food supports expansion, timber enables construction, and ore enables better tools.
- **Fixed upgrade paths:** Progress depends on acquiring resources and purchasing upgrades; building placement does not optimize output.
- **Long-term expansion:** Additional archetypes and regions introduce capabilities and continued uses for earlier resources.
- **Active gathering:** Archetype-specific minigames let players help produce the resource they currently need. These should be enjoyable games with meaningful skill and clear round endings.
- **Active upgrades:** Gathering equipment develops separately from passive production. Active rewards remain useful as the economy grows; exact curves are undecided.
- **Idle expeditions:** Commit resources to timed combat expeditions for rewards and opportunities. The prototype represents these with a simple timer.
- **An immersive browser interface:** The settlement is the main menu. Players select buildings in the scene to manage production, buy upgrades, or begin activities.

## What remains open

Art direction, the final resource catalog, progression curves, deeper minigame mechanics, expedition combat, regional expansion, and the eventual scale of the world remain design work. The current prototype demonstrates the interaction loop and is not a commitment to its balance or visual style.
